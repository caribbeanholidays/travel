Who We Are

Caribbean Holidays — Your Kashmir Gateway

Caribbean Holidays is a premier tour operator based in the heart of Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. We specialise in crafting personalised Kashmir travel experiences that go beyond just a trip — we create stories worth telling.

Founded with a deep passion for Kashmir's unparalleled beauty, our team brings together local expertise, genuine hospitality, and a commitment to making every traveller feel at home in the Valley of Paradise.
